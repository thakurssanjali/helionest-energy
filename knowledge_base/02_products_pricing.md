# Products and pricing

## Pricing basics
All prices are indicative and include GST, installation, standard mounting structure, wiring, earthing, lightning arrester and the net-meter application.
The final price is confirmed in a written quote after the free site survey.
Under the government scheme, the customer pays the full system price first. The subsidy is later credited by the government directly to the customer's bank account.

## On-grid home solar (most popular)
An on-grid system is connected to the electricity grid with a net meter. Extra units go to the grid and reduce the bill.
It has no battery, so it does NOT give power during a power cut.

## Hybrid solar with battery backup
A hybrid system saves on the bill like on-grid AND gives backup during power cuts.
- Hybrid inverter upgrade: ₹25,000 extra over the on-grid price.
- 5 kWh lithium battery: ₹65,000 extra. It runs fans, lights, fridge and Wi-Fi for about 6 to 8 hours.
- 10 kWh lithium battery: ₹1,20,000 extra.
- The government subsidy applies only to the solar panel capacity, not to batteries or the hybrid inverter.

## How to choose the right system size
- In Punjab and North India, 1 kW of solar generates roughly 100 to 140 units per month. We use 120 units per kW per month for estimates (less in winter, more in summer).
- Quick rule: divide the monthly bill by 7 to get approximate units, then divide units by 120 to get the kW needed.
- Example: a ₹2,500 monthly bill is about 350 units, so a 3 kW system fits.
- Roof space needed: about 100 square feet of shadow-free roof per kW. A 3 kW system needs about 300 square feet.
- If the system is bigger than the sanctioned load of the connection, a load increase may be needed. We help with this application.

## Savings and payback estimate
- We assume an average tariff of about ₹7 per unit.
- A 3 kW system makes about 360 units per month, saving roughly ₹2,500 per month or ₹30,000 per year.
- After the subsidy, a typical home system pays for itself in about 3.5 to 4 years.
- Solar panels last 25 years or more.
