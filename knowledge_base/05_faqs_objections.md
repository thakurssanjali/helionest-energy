# Frequently asked questions and objection handling

## Does solar work in winter, fog or rain?
Yes, but it produces less. On cloudy or foggy days generation can drop to about half. Over a full year, a system averages about 120 units per kW per month in North India.

## Will I get electricity during a power cut?
Not with an on-grid system. For safety, on-grid systems switch off when the grid is down. If the customer wants backup during power cuts, recommend a hybrid system with a battery.

## How much roof space do I need?
About 100 square feet of shadow-free roof per kW. A 3 kW system needs about 300 square feet.

## Which roof types work?
Flat concrete (RCC) roofs are best. Tin or sheet roofs and sloped roofs also work with special mounting.

## What maintenance is needed?
Just wash the dust off the panels with plain water every 2 to 3 weeks. There is no other regular maintenance. We also offer AMC plans.

## I live in a rented house. Can I get solar?
The subsidy must be applied for by the person whose name is on the electricity connection, usually the owner. We are happy to speak with the house owner.

## What happens to the extra electricity?
The net meter records units sent to the grid, and the DISCOM adjusts them against the units used, as per the state's net-metering rules.

## Is it safe? What about lightning?
Yes. Every installation includes proper earthing and a lightning arrester.

## How long do the panels last?
Panels last 25 years or more and have a 25-year performance warranty.

## Objection: "It is too expensive."
Explain that the subsidy reduces the cost by up to ₹78,000 (₹85,800 in Himachal Pradesh and J&K). EMI is about ₹1,100 per month per lakh, which is often less than the monthly bill saving. Payback is about 3.5 to 4 years, and the panels last 25 years.

## Objection: "I will think about it."
That is completely fine. Suggest the free, no-obligation site survey so they get exact numbers for their home. Mention the winter offer valid till 31 December 2026.

## Objection: "Another company is cheaper."
Ask what is included in the other quote. Compare: DCR panels (needed for the subsidy), warranties, paperwork handled, and after-sales service. Never speak badly about competitors. Offer a callback from a human manager to review the other quote.

## Objection: "I don't trust that the subsidy will really come."
The subsidy is paid by the government directly into the customer's bank account through the national portal. HelioNest handles the paperwork, and we have done more than 2,400 installations.

## Objection: "My bill is very low."
Be honest. If the monthly bill is below about ₹800, solar may not save much. A 1 kW system could still help, but there is no pressure.

## Objection: "Call me later."
Ask for a convenient day and time, and note it as a callback request.
