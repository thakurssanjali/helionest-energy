# Installation process, warranties and service

## Step-by-step installation process
1. Free site survey within 48 hours of booking (about 45 minutes): roof measurement, shadow check, load check and bill review.
2. Written proposal and quote within 24 hours of the survey.
3. Booking and registration: we register the customer on the PM Surya Ghar portal and apply to the DISCOM for feasibility approval (7 to 15 days).
4. Installation: 1 to 3 days depending on system size.
5. Net metering: the DISCOM installs the net meter (typically 7 to 20 days).
6. Inspection and commissioning, then the subsidy is credited to the customer's bank account (about 30 days).
In total it usually takes 4 to 6 weeks from booking to subsidy.

## Free site survey details
- The site survey is free and has no obligation to buy.
- Available slots: Monday to Saturday at 10 AM, 12 PM, 2 PM and 4 PM.
- To book we need the customer's name, phone number and full address with city.
- On the day of the survey, keep a recent electricity bill ready (a photo is fine).
- The house owner or decision maker should be present.
- Customers can reschedule or cancel the survey anytime by calling us.

## What is included in a home installation
- DCR mono-crystalline solar panels (540 to 580 watt each).
- On-grid inverter (or hybrid inverter for battery systems).
- Galvanised steel mounting structure. An elevated structure (for using the roof space below) costs ₹5,000 per kW extra.
- DC and AC cabling, earthing, lightning arrester, net-meter application and the generation-tracking app.

## Warranties
- Solar panels: 12-year product warranty and 25-year performance warranty from the manufacturer.
- On-grid inverter: 7 years. Hybrid inverter: 5 years.
- Lithium battery: 5 years.
- HelioNest workmanship (structure, wiring, roof leakage due to installation): 5 years.

## After-sales service
- Complaint helpline Monday to Saturday.
- Urgent issues get a response within 24 hours and are usually fixed within 72 hours.
- The app alerts the customer and our team if generation drops.
- Maintenance is free for the first year.

## When to transfer to a human
A human team member calls back within 2 working hours when:
- the caller is upset or asks to speak to a human,
- the caller has a complaint about an existing installation,
- the caller has a commercial project above 50 kW,
- the caller asks for a discount beyond the current offer,
- the caller has legal or contract questions.

## Privacy and data handling
- We only collect the name, phone number, city or address and electricity bill details needed for the enquiry.
- We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, card numbers or OTPs over a call.
- Customers can ask us to stop calling them or to delete their data at any time.
