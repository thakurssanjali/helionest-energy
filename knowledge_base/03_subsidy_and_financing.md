# Government subsidy and financing

## PM Surya Ghar subsidy amounts
PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana is the central government scheme for residential rooftop solar. As per current guidelines it runs till 31 March 2027.
- Most states, including Punjab, Haryana and Chandigarh: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW, and ₹18,000 for the third kW.
  - 1 kW: ₹30,000. 2 kW: ₹60,000. 3 kW or bigger: ₹78,000 (maximum).
- Special category states and UTs, including Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Uttarakhand and the North-East: ₹33,000 per kW for the first 2 kW, and ₹19,800 for the third kW.
  - 1 kW: ₹33,000. 2 kW: ₹66,000. 3 kW or bigger: ₹85,800 (maximum).
- Systems bigger than 3 kW get the same maximum subsidy as a 3 kW system.
- Housing societies and RWAs get ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities.
- Some states add their own subsidy on top of the central subsidy. Our team checks this during the site survey.
- Subsidy amounts follow current government guidelines and can change. Final eligibility is decided by the national portal (pmsuryaghar.gov.in) and the electricity distribution company (DISCOM).

## How and when the subsidy is paid
- The customer pays the full system price to HelioNest.
- After installation, net metering and inspection, the government credits the subsidy directly to the customer's bank account.
- This usually takes about 30 days after commissioning (indicative).
- HelioNest does all the portal registration and paperwork for the customer.

## Who is eligible for the subsidy
- The electricity connection must be a residential connection in the applicant's own name.
- The applicant must own the house and have a suitable roof.
- The applicant must not have taken any other solar subsidy before.
- The panels must be DCR (made-in-India). All HelioNest home systems use DCR panels.
- Tenants cannot apply themselves. The house owner must apply.

## Loans and EMI options
- Collateral-free solar loans are available through our partner banks under the scheme. The interest rate is decided by the bank.
- Indicative EMI: about ₹1,100 per month for every ₹1 lakh borrowed over 10 years (at around 6% interest).
- No-cost EMI on credit cards for 3 to 12 months through our payment partner.
- Our team helps the customer with the loan paperwork.

## Payment terms
- 10% booking amount after the customer accepts the written quote.
- 80% before the material is dispatched.
- 10% after installation and commissioning.
- Accepted: UPI, net banking, cheque and cards.
- HelioNest never asks for an OTP, PIN, card number or bank password over a phone call.
