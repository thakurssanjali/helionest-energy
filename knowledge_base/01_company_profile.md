# HelioNest Energy Pvt. Ltd. — Company profile

## About HelioNest Energy
HelioNest Energy Pvt. Ltd. is a rooftop solar company headquartered in Jalandhar, Punjab, founded in 2019.
We design, install and maintain rooftop solar systems for homes, housing societies, shops, schools and small factories.
We have completed more than 2,400 installations (about 9 MW in total) and have a team of 60 people, including 35 certified installation engineers.
We are a registered vendor for the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana rooftop solar scheme.

## Mission
Help every North Indian home and business cut its electricity bill with clean, reliable solar power, with honest advice and no hidden costs.
